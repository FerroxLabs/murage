Restore from a backup

A backup holds your settings, bots, conversations, files and channel history. It does not hold sign-ins to AI engines or messaging apps, the local VM, or folders outside Murage. A restore goes into a new folder and stays paused until you review it; you connect engines and messaging apps again afterwards.

You need the backup file (it ends in .age) and your recovery key (murage-recovery-key.txt, or your copy of it). Without the recovery key a backup can't be opened.

This data folder was last used on another computer

Leave the folder and its files as they are, including any lock files. Renaming a computer and moving Murage to a new computer are fixed in different ways.

Open the diagnostics folder and contact support with the two computer names shown here. Don't send your conversations, keys or the whole data folder. To move Murage to a new computer, make a backup on the old one and restore it here.

Recover without a backup

Make a recovery copy from the data on this computer. Windows asks once for administrator approval. Your current data stays as it is.

The copy is taken at one moment, so unfinished work may be missing. It opens paused for your review, and you connect engines and apps again.

Restore a backup into a new folder

Restore a Murage backup into a new folder on this computer. Your current data stays as it is.

Only what is in the backup comes back. Anything newer in your current data is not copied. Leave your current data and its lock files as they are.

Older recovery files (.zip)

Murage used to make unencrypted .zip recovery files that hold less than a backup. You can still restore one. Your current data is kept, and restored bots stay paused.

Backup details for support

After the restore, review the restored copy before opening it. AI engines and schedules stay off until you turn them on.

Review the restored copy

Murage checks the restored copy and shows what comes back switched off. If anything changes after you review it, you review it again.

Other recovery options
