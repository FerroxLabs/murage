## PURPOSE
An owner's thread keeps its engine and its conversation from one turn to the next, on macOS, Linux and Windows, so turn 2 and later
start from a hot process with resumed=true (about 1 s), and a cold start still resumes the conversation instead of losing it. Memory
and permission safety stay exactly as strong as today.

## USER PROMISES
- P1 WARM: on an owner thread, turn N+1 after a normal turn N logs `process=reused` AND `resumed=true` in the same server run, unless a
  named cold cause applies: model/tools/connectors changed, CLI version changed, Stop, crash, memory reset on that session, an in-process
  grant (R5), S2-R back-off active, or a pool limit: at most 3 idle owner engines per engine kind while the owner is active, none after
  15 minutes with no owner turn (activity window, unchanged), and the pool memory budget (min(25% of RAM, RAM - 6 GB), warm-pool.ts:324-329;
  6144 MB on a 24 GB machine). Every cold turn logs its cause.
- P2 RESUMED AFTER COLD: when a new process is needed (restart, crash, update, limit), the turn still resumes (`resumed=true`) unless
  memory safety requires that conversation to go.
- P3 MEMORY: an engine never keeps, or carries into a later turn, content the owner deleted or revoked. A session that may hold a revoked
  line resets once. Nothing remembered is added to a turn whose check is unfinished.
- P4 PERMISSION: every tool call in a later turn is judged against the owner's CURRENT grants; nothing granted in-process in an earlier
  turn, and nothing started by an earlier turn's tool, keeps working in a later turn.
- P5 STOP AND HONESTY: Stop, crash and refusal behave as today (JOINT RULING STOP-VS-REFUSAL unchanged, incl. Stop during the barrier).
  No new prompts, no limits on the owner (No limits rule), no telemetry. Warmth is a speed-up only: it never refuses or delays a turn
  beyond the barrier deadline, and a failed reuse runs cold for the same turn.
- P6 HELD THREAD (new): a thread with an unfinished deletion resumes its own conversation. A retained session keeps running with only the
  held lines out; a fresh replay withholds every generated line from the hold's since until the drain ends (deny-read.ts:147-150), so on a
  fresh process more than the held lines may be out until then [R2.1-G]. When a turn runs
  with held lines out, the owner sees one quiet line "Still checking a deletion" (no card, no button, no content named), at most once per
  thread until the hold set changes.
