## 0c: HL-LOCATOR-1 / creation evidence / checker owner adoption

`recovery-contract.ts` publishes strict `LocatorMetadata`, `LocatorPayload`, `SealedLocator`, `LocatorReservation`, opaque `LocatorAllocation` / `LocatorAccessAuthority`, and the `RecoveryLocatorStorage` / `RecoveryLocatorLifecycle` interfaces. This is an independent deletable store contract, not a TTL on approval/RPC journals. Maximum lifetime is 86,400,000 ms from initial preparation; reads/retries/restarts never renew it. Bound metadata authenticates approval/request/authority/plan/operation/location/connection epoch/credential version/conversation, random reference, epoch key reference, keyed HMAC commitment and created/expiry times. The journal plan keeps only the bounded reference/commitment/times and effect digests, never selector ciphertext or plaintext. The time helper is a necessary gate, never authorization or ciphertext authentication.

Only minimum qualified email/phone/name selectors (including an explicit identity-rule digest) or F10 approved old/next domain/path are admitted. Email equality/name-query bounds are 75 characters; no truncation, query trimming or silent replacement is performed. A longer unsupported selector requires another independently approved qualified locator or an explicit pre-credential refusal. No message/note/task body, arbitrary review values, provider response, tokens or plaintext spill is accepted. Payload UTF-8 JSON is ≤4 KiB. The sealed representation fixes AES-256-GCM with a 12-byte nonce, ciphertext including its tag, and the full metadata as authenticated AAD. Shape parsing is not a cryptographic validation claim. Epoch keys are independent random bank entries owned by C1; no plaintext key sibling or reproducible retired key.

Reserve only nonsecret capacity/index metadata in held memory with original preparation time. Admission counts unresolved reservations/allocations toward the bounded capacity; it never evicts an older unresolved selector. Once the exact approval exists: commit allocation plus all cleanup indexes durably, publish authenticated ciphertext atomically, activate the matching bound plan, then permit dispatch. Incomplete allocations remain indexed jobs. Per-location/epoch, per-conversation and due-time indexes are mandatory. At most 256 active payloads; a tick examines at most 32 indexed entries and one bounded index page. Startup cleanup resumes independently of replay/approval history. Already-expired/revoked records cannot be opened even with a cleanup backlog.

Terminal settlement, cancel/stale, applicable grant revoke, connection replacement/disconnect and conversation deletion revoke eligibility before acknowledgement and enqueue cleanup. Current epoch, grant and conversation liveness, authenticated times, metadata integrity and key availability are checked before decryption/key/credential access. Clock rollback before createdAt refuses. On deletion, retain job/index until unlink and directory durability complete, then remove indexes. A crash resumes that same job. Disconnect retires the epoch key before dropping the bank. Held cleanup remains visible to Doctor; there is no false completed-deletion report. After locator loss/expiry/revoke, actions with no remaining approved direct-ID evidence stay read-free unknown and non-dispatchable; never ask replay args to reconstruct the old selector. New requested work is a new approval. Permanent replay tombstones remain immutable, do not expire, and reject selector/ciphertext/reversible copies.

Creation evidence is the strict minimal `{approvalId, requestDigest, authorityDigest, operation, credentialVersion, recordId, creationBranch, confirmationDigest}` record from an authenticated successful response to this exact dispatch, validated against held request/authority/raw identities/confirmation and persisted before terminal settlement/result release. Upserts preserve the provider's created/existing discriminator. `DispatchEvidencePublisher` owns the opaque validated-record capability and checked reload. Search absence, a complete pre-search, a unique post-search same-effect record, timestamps and synthesized `new:true` cannot fill it. Even a durable response ID still needs the independent fresh exact-ID/full-effect recovery gate; a normal direct dispatch confirmation may settle normally. Delayed-index creation remains unknown with no resend.
