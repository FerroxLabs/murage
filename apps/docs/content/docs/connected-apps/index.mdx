Open **Connected apps** from the sidebar (**Sidebar → Apps**, in the row under Needs you), choose a service, and sign in to it in your normal browser. Murage then lets your bots use that service, on every engine that supports connected apps.

<ProductScreenshot name="docs-connected-apps" alt="The clean Murage connected-app marketplace" caption="Search the marketplace or review every account from the Connected tab." />

## Connecting an app

1. Open **Sidebar → Apps** (Connected apps).
2. Search for Gmail, GitHub, Slack, Notion, or another app.
3. Select **Connect**.
4. Sign in on the service's own page in the browser window that opens. Murage never sees your password.
5. Return to the app and ask a bot to use the service.

Connected apps run through Flux Router. Add your Flux Router key under **Settings → Models & engines** and the apps unlock. There is no separate key to find or paste.

Murage keeps its own sign-in in your computer's secure storage. Your sign-ins to each service stay with the connected-apps service: they are never put into a bot's instructions or shown on screen.

## If you used a key of your own before

Connected apps no longer use a key you saved yourself. Your saved value is kept, untouched, but nothing reads it. Connect your apps again through Flux Router from the Connected apps page.

## Letting a bot use connected apps

Each bot has a switch for connected apps: **Bot settings → Access → Allow this bot to use connected apps**. What shapes (bot name) shows whether connected apps are part of what the bot can use.

## Bots stay connected

Each bot checks the connected-apps service again before every reply, so your bots get their apps back on their own after a short network drop. If a bot says it has no connected apps, see **Troubleshooting → Connected apps**.

## Multiple accounts

Connect more than one account for the same app and give each a clear alias such as `work`, `personal`, or `client-acme`. When several accounts could perform an action, Murage requires explicit selection instead of silently replacing the first connection.

## Disconnecting

Disconnecting an account takes back the permission you gave that service. It does not only hide the account in Murage. Other accounts for the same app stay connected.
