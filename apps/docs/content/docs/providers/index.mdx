Murage does not send every message through its own AI account. It starts the AI apps you installed on this computer, such as Claude or Codex, and shows their work in one chat.

<ProductScreenshot name="docs-engine-detection" alt="Murage checking which AI apps are installed" caption="Murage checks what is installed and signed in for you. You do not need to find any files." />

## Built-in engines

Each engine is an AI app on this computer. You sign in to it once, with your own account, and Murage uses that sign-in.

| Engine | How you sign in | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Claude | Sign in to the Claude app | Strong support for tools, approvals, saved sessions and using this computer |
| Codex | Sign in to the Codex app | Lists its models, asks for approval, takes new directions mid-task, uses computer tools |
| Grok | Sign in to the Grok app | Lists its models and keeps sessions |
| Cursor | Sign in to the Cursor Agent app, or use your own Cursor key | Lists its models as they change |
| Kimi | Sign in to the Kimi app | Works through the common AI app connection |
| Droid | Sign in to the Factory Droid app | Works through the common AI app connection |
| Antigravity | Sign in to the Google Antigravity app | The default engine for a personal Google account |
| OpenCode | Your existing OpenCode sign-in, or your own OpenCode key | Lists Zen, Go, other services' and local models |
| Qwen | Sign in to the Qwen app | Works through the common AI app connection |
| Hermes | Sign in to the Hermes app | Works through the common AI app connection |
| Pi | Sign in to the Pi app | Built-in connection |

The "common AI app connection" is a shared language Murage uses to talk to many AI apps the same way. An engine that is not ready stays in the list with the reason and the next step, and the others keep working. If you run Murage from source, you can add more engines in its settings file.

## Use a plan you already have

Sign in with the plan you already pay for and your bots can use the models that come with it. Open **Settings → Models & engines** in the Murage desktop app and press Continue with ChatGPT or Continue with Grok under Use a plan you already have. Plan limits apply. On a phone or in a browser, finish sign-in on the computer running Murage. The Grok sign-in is marked Unofficial: xAI has not published it for other apps yet, it needs a SuperGrok or X Premium plan, and an xAI API key works instead if it is ever turned off.

## Grok images

Bots can make and edit images with Grok. Add an xAI key under Models & engines, then open **Settings → Tools → Image generation**, pick the xAI connection and choose Grok Imagine Image 2.0. xAI has no default image model, so choose it yourself.

## Detection

Murage looks for your AI apps in the usual places, in the background. After you install an AI app or sign in to it, restart Murage.

If Murage still cannot find one, open **Settings → Models & engines** and use **Choose location…** on that engine to point it at the app. This works for every engine and helps when you keep several versions, use a version manager or a wrapper, or installed the app somewhere unusual on Windows.

## Model selection

The model picker groups models by the AI service they come from and marks the defaults. Engines that support it can refresh their list of models from the app. A new model choice applies to the next replies; earlier messages stay as they were.
