## Image attachments

Paste a screenshot directly into the composer or attach an image from disk. Add a short instruction explaining what the agent should inspect: the highlighted UI, an error message, a layout mismatch, or the whole image.

## Search

The sidebar search finds bots and conversation content. Use distinctive project names, issue numbers, or exact error fragments to narrow results quickly.

## Message actions

- **Pin** durable decisions or instructions.
- **Copy** a response without selecting the message manually.
- **React** to leave lightweight feedback in shared conversations.
- **Retry** when a short problem with your AI service or your connection stops a response.
- **Read aloud** when voice output is configured.

<Callout type="info" title="Screenshots may contain secrets">
  Crop out keys, sign-in codes, email addresses, private project names, and customer data before attaching an image to any cloud-backed model.
</Callout>

## Search all chats

Open **All chats** from the sidebar, press Cmd+Shift+K (Ctrl+Shift+K on Windows and Linux), or choose Expand on a bot's or channel's chat list to see just those chats. Every chat you have, newest first and grouped by day, is in one list.

Type in **Search all chats**. The list narrows by title. From three characters, Murage also searches your messages and shows Matches in messages, one row per chat; open one and you land on the message.

Narrow further with Bot, Project or channel, Date (Today, Past week, Past month or a custom range), Pinned, Unread, Archived and Include routine runs. Clear filters resets them. A line you chose to forget never shows up in a match.
