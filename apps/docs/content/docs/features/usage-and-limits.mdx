## Plan usage

**Settings > Usage > Plan usage** shows one row per engine account with what the engine last reported about its plan limits: the percent used, when the period resets, and when Murage last checked. Press Refresh to check again. If an account has reached its limit, the row says so.

Plan usage appears when the engine reports it, which today is Codex. Until then the card says "Plan usage not available yet". On Cloud it reads "Plan usage is not reported on Cloud yet."

## Monthly limit

**Settings > Usage > Monthly limit** shows what Flux billed this month against a limit you choose. The field starts at the suggested $1,000 a month, and nothing is applied until you press Save limit. Remove limit clears it.

## The heads-up on Today

With a limit set, Today shows "Chat spend through Flux" as an amount of your limit and a percent. At 80% and again at the limit, Today gives you a heads-up with Raise limit or Leave it. It is a heads-up only, and your bots keep working.

The figure counts chat use through Flux. It comes from Flux itself, can trail by a few minutes, and follows Flux's billing month. When your bots use your own sign-in or key, that provider bills you and its own limits apply.
