## What shapes a bot

Every bot's window has a section called **What shapes (bot name)**, the third section in Bot settings. It lists everything that goes into that bot's instructions, grouped, in the order the model reads them. Desktop app only.

The groups are:

- **Your rules**: House rules, which always come first.
- **Who it is**: its description, personality, team brief, its notebook, and About me.
- **What it can use**: its computer, connected apps, browser, web search and each skill.
- **This turn**: routines, output folder, and the date and time.

## Switching parts off

Parts you chose can be switched off from here: House rules, About me, the team brief, the Chief of Staff guide and each of the bot's own skills. A switched-off row says "Off. (bot) doesn't read this now." Each row has View, and an Edit link that takes you to where the part is written (Edit in Settings for House rules and About me, Open in Skills for skills).

Rows with a lock are Murage's own rules. They say "Always on" and cannot be switched off.

## Show exactly what it read

Press **Show exactly what it read** to see the bot's full instructions from its last reply, word for word, with the time it read them. If the bot has not replied yet, send it a message and look again.
