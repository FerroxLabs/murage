## Calm and Detailed

Today opens with a Calm view: a short summary of your day. Use the switch at the top right, Calm or Detailed, to choose how much Today shows. Murage remembers your choice.

Calm shows a greeting and a one-line summary, a box to tell your Chief what you need, up to three lines in Needs you, Your day (now to midnight), Working now and Done today, then your team.

## What Detailed adds

Detailed shows every line in Needs you, a Reported routine cost card, a line about the routines that ran, every run in Done today with how long it took and the engine it ran on, and Your team today in place of the team chips.

The switch is not shown in first run. If saving your choice does not go through, Today says "That did not save. Try again."
