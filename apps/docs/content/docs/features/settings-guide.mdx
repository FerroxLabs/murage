## Two kinds of settings

Settings for the whole app open from your menu in the sidebar. Settings for one bot open from that bot, under Bot settings. If a setting is not on either list, it does not exist. See If something isn't possible.

## App settings: You and Bots

**You**: General (your profile, and how the app looks and behaves) and About me (what every bot should know about you). **Bots**: Bot defaults (how new bots start, and how bots take turns in channels and projects), House rules (rules every bot follows), Skills (know-how your bots can use) and Memory (what your bots remember between conversations).

## App settings: Models and Tools

**Models**: Models (the AI models your bots think with, and their keys) and Engines (the programs on this computer that run your bots). **Tools**: Images (let bots make and edit pictures), Web search, Voice (turn speech into text for recorded skills), Connected apps (Gmail, Slack, Notion and others) and Computer & browser.

## App settings: Messaging and App

**Messaging & phone**: Messaging apps (Telegram, Slack and Discord, see Telegram, Slack and Discord) and Phone and other devices (see The Murage phone app). **App**: Backups, Usage (how much each bot has used), Help & updates, and Experimental (early features you can try, which may change).

## Bot settings

Open a bot, then Bot settings. Overview (profile, avatar and Role). Identity & instructions (name, title, description, personality). What shapes this bot (everything the bot reads, in order). Skills. Memory. Routines. Teams (sharing the bot with other teams). Access (computer, browser, connected apps, working folder).

## Bot settings: the rest

Model (engine and effort). Permissions (the approval level, Ask me before contacting other bots, Can talk to, Full access, and images). Voice & alerts (the bot's voice and notifications). History (tasks and conversations). Usage (turns and tokens). Learning (what the bot has learned from you).

## Places in the sidebar

The sidebar has Routines (things your bots do on a schedule), Files (everything your bots made or saved), Apps (connected apps) and Team map (who is on which team and which bots have handed work to which). Your own menu opens About me, What's new and Settings.
