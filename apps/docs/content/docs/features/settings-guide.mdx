## Two kinds of settings

Settings for the whole app open from your menu in the sidebar. Settings for one bot open from that bot, under Bot settings. If a setting is not on either list, it does not exist. See If something isn't possible.

## App settings: You and Bots

**You**: General (your profile, About me, and how the app looks and behaves) and Calls. **All bots**: How every bot behaves (including House rules), Skills (know-how your bots can use) and Memory (what your bots remember between conversations). **Each bot** opens settings for one bot.

## App settings: Models and Tools

**AI**: Models & engines (the AI models, their keys and the programs that run your bots) and Search, images and dictation (web search, image generation, voice notes and transcription).

## App settings: Messaging and App

**Reach**: Connected apps (including Telegram, Slack, Discord and WhatsApp), Computer & browser, and Phone and other devices. **App**: Backups, Usage (how much each bot has used) and About & help (updates, Murage news and help).

## Moving in from another app

If the first-run screen finds Grok Bot data on this computer, **Move my bots** imports it; **Not now** leaves it alone. Under Settings → General, **Bring what you already use** opens the Grok Bot import or **Learn about me**. Opening either option does not import anything; you review and choose what to bring over.

## Built-in browser

The built-in browser is on by default. Its workspace switch lives in **Settings → Computer & browser → Built-in browser**. Each bot also has its own browser switch in Bot settings → Access. A switch you turned off stays off. Bots still hand steps such as entering credentials or agreeing to terms back to you.

## Teach a skill

Teach a skill is on by default in the desktop app. Record a workflow, review the result and choose which bots can use it. Find its switch in **Settings → Skills → Teach a skill**, and open the recorder from your menu in the sidebar. A switch you turned off stays off. Recording is available on your computer; Cloud homes keep the skills library without the recorder.

## Bot settings

Open a bot, then Bot settings. Overview (profile, avatar and Role). Identity & instructions (name, title, description, personality). What shapes this bot (everything the bot reads, in order). Skills. Memory. Routines. Teams (sharing the bot with other teams). Access (computer, browser, connected apps, working folder).

## Bot settings: the rest

Model (engine and effort). Permissions (the approval level, Ask me before contacting other bots, Can talk to, Full access, and images). Voice & alerts (the bot's voice and notifications). History (tasks and conversations). Usage (turns and tokens). Learning (what the bot has learned from you).

## Places in the sidebar

The sidebar has Routines (things your bots do on a schedule), Files (everything your bots made or saved), Apps (connected apps) and Team map (who is on which team and which bots have handed work to which). Your own menu opens About me, What's new and Settings.
