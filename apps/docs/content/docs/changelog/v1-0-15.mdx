## New bots in 1.0.15

- **Start on Fuigo.** A new bot you have not picked an engine for starts on Fuigo when Fuigo can answer, and otherwise on the engine Murage found on this computer, so your first chat gets a reply.
- **No silent waits.** If Fuigo goes quiet partway through an answer, Murage ends that turn and tells you right away, so you can send it again.

## Answer in one place in 1.0.15

- **One line in the chat.** A bot waiting on you shows one line in the chat. Answer it in the panel above the box, or from the bar on your phone.
- Messages waiting for the next turn fold to one tidy line each.

## Less in your way in 1.0.15

- **All chats.** All chats has Back and a close button at the top right, like every other page.
- **Esc.** Esc closes the menu or panel in front first. Health closes with Back, its close button or Esc.
- **Opens faster.** Panels you have not opened yet load when you first open them.

## Better in 1.0.15

- Memories keep what you said, not tool output.
- A message from one bot to another leaves the memory of that pair's room as it was.
- Deleting a chat leaves work running in your other chats alone.
- Use my Chrome offers Set up my browser first, and leaving it hands the browser back at once, even while a bot is using it.
- On Grok Build, a question from an MCP server opens as a question card.
- Plus stability fixes.
