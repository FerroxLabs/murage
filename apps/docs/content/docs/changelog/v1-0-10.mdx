## Full access and No limits in 1.0.10

- **Your level means it.** At No limits and Full access, your bots run their routines and your own direct messages at the level you chose, without stopping to ask.
- **Routines keep your level.** A routine runs at the level you chose, and so do the bots it hands work to and the follow-up turns it wakes.
- **Your own messages count as you.** Direct messages you send your bot from your own Telegram, Slack or Discord run at the bot's level. You can turn this off in Settings.
- **Long writes run straight through.** Long file writes run at No limits and Full access without a card.
- **The never-list still stands.** No limits still stops for the short never-list, such as erasing a disk, and before a bot reads a key file.

## Connected apps in 1.0.10

- **Steady on a patchy connection.** Murage tries again before it calls your connected apps out of reach, so your routines keep their apps.
