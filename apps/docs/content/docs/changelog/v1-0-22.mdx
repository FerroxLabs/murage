Murage 1.0.22 is out for Mac, Windows and Linux. Your computer stays calm while Murage works in the background, memory search reaches further back, and your bots keep answering through long days.

## New in 1.0.22

- **Memory search finds older memories.** Ask for something you saved a while ago and your bot finds it, even in a very long history. The Memory page tells you when a search covered everything.
- **Faster startup.** Murage gets to your chats sooner on every launch, with less work done up front.
- **Approve works the first time.** The Approve button and one-tap answers in Needs you land on the first click, and a request that has ended is shown as ended.
- **Replies keep coming in long conversations.** Even when a conversation grows large, your bots carry on and rebuild what they need so you still get your answer.
- **Bots with full access keep going.** After you answer a card in your own thread, the bot picks up right where it left off at its full access level.

## Better in 1.0.22

- **Quiet on your disk and processor.** While you are away, Murage does very little background work, and the jobs that matter still run on time.
- **Background work rests when there is nothing to do.** Murage's background checks wake only when there is something to look at, so your computer stays cool.
- **Your storage stays tidy.** Old checkpoints and old upgrade copies are cleared on a sensible schedule, and restoring a checkpoint never touches files it did not save.
- **Lighter channels.** Signal, sharing and Release cards do less work behind the scenes.

## How to update in 1.0.22

Murage shows an update banner when 1.0.22 is ready, or choose Check for updates in Settings.
