## Tools on OpenAI-compatible engines in 1.0.9

- **Tools on every OpenAI-compatible engine.** Connect any OpenAI-compatible engine and your bots use their tools there too, with the same approval cards and the same Stop as every other engine.

## Your phone in 1.0.9

- **Bot calls.** When something needs you, your bot can call your phone. The lock screen only says Murage is calling; answer to hear what it is, or choose Not now. Calls off for today is in Settings, Notifications, Calls.
- **A phone your bots can use.** When you ask, bots can check your calendar and add events, read and add reminders, set alarms and timers, find a contact to start a text or a call, and find things nearby. Each request shows a card on the phone for you to approve.
- **Share to Murage and Ask Murage.** Share links, photos and files from any app straight into a chat with a bot, or ask your bots from anywhere on the phone by voice or with one tap. On Android, Murage can be your assistant app.
- **Your phone stays connected.** Your phone stays connected to Murage through short network hiccups.

## Health and backups in 1.0.9

- **Health checks.** A Health card in the Inbox and in Settings shows what Murage's checks found, with one short daily summary when something changed. The checks read only health figures, never your conversations or memories.
- **Backups include your cards.** Backups include your question and approval cards, along with everything else Murage keeps.
- **Forget means gone.** Forget clears a memory's words from search as well, even across large deletions.

## Full access and No limits in 1.0.9

- **No limits runs your own scripts straight away**, on Mac, Windows and Linux.
- **Fewer cards at Full access.** More everyday compound commands run straight through without a card.
- **Turns run on every computer.** Where Murage can't add its power protection, the turn still runs.

## Everyday use in 1.0.9

- **Bots connect to the services you choose.** Ask a bot to use an API and it can build the connection itself and keep the key you give it.
- **Questions from your tools.** When a connected tool needs an answer from you, Claude asks you right in the chat and carries on once you reply.
- **Grok sees your pictures.** Grok sees the pictures you send, and a message you send mid-reply joins the turn that is running.
