## Conversations in 1.0.5

- **Deleting a conversation finishes on its own.** Murage keeps working through a deletion until every engine's copy is gone, and the notice tells you what is still on the way.

## Connected apps in 1.0.5

- **Connected apps tell you when they need you.** When an app needs to be connected again, Murage shows it in the chat, with the step to bring it back.

## Teams in 1.0.5

- **Hand-offs name what held them up.** When a teammate cannot pick up a hand-off, the project's Activity says why, so you know what to change.
