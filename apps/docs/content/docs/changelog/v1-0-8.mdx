## Fewer interruptions at Full access in 1.0.8

- **Fewer approval cards.** At Full access and No limits, your bots ask far less often before everyday commands, most of all on Windows.
- **Everyday commands keep flowing on Windows.** They keep going even when Murage's check of your computer's setup is slow or doesn't finish.
- **One default for every conversation.** Raise your default and Murage offers to apply it to your open conversations too, with a count of how many it will change.
- **No limits in project rooms.** No limits in a project room stays on after Murage restarts.

## Stop in 1.0.8

- **Stop that lands cleanly.** On Linux, Stop gives sandboxed engines a moment to finish up and tidy away their work, so the next turn picks up right where you left off.

## Backups in 1.0.8

- **Backups you can count on.** If Murage can't save its backup state, it tells you exactly what to check (disk space and permissions), and Return to workspace takes you straight back once it's sorted.

## Your computer and your secrets in 1.0.8

- **Your computer stays yours.** On Windows, macOS and Linux x64, engines Murage wraps cannot shut down or restart your computer.
- **Your secrets stay hidden.** Keys, tokens and private key blocks are masked in the error messages Murage shows you, from engines, computers, checkpoints and updates.
- **Deleting a chat clears Fuigo's copy.** On Ubuntu 22.04 and Debian 12, deleting a chat also clears the copy Fuigo keeps on that computer, in under a second.

## Everyday use in 1.0.8

- **The built-in browser is on for everyone.** On Mac, Teach a skill is ready to use from the start.
- **Helper bots carry on.** Approvals a helper bot asks for reach you and carry its work on once you answer.
- **One update control.** The sidebar has a single update control: one spinner while a new version downloads, and a tap opens the update details.
- **Every reply explains itself.** When a reply can't finish, or ends with nothing to show, Murage tells you why in one plain line.
