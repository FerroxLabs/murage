## No limits in 1.0.18

- **Your bot gets on with the job.** At No limits, when you start the request yourself, a bot runs what you asked.
- **A protected list.** Murage protects certain files and places, such as the keys and sign-ins you have given it, and answers a request for one with a clear refusal that says why.
- **Outside content.** If a bot has taken in something from outside, such as a web page, an email or a file someone sent, only that bot keeps the extra check.
- **Mac temp folders.** Commands in your Mac temp folders run as usual, and the real system folders stay protected.
- **Small everyday commands.** Writing output to /dev/null works, and a trivial inline check, such as a one-number node or python command, runs straight away.

## Today and chats in 1.0.18

- **Today, two ways.** The Today page has a Calm view for a short summary and a Detailed view for the full list, in every language.
- **Search all chats.** One search box looks through every chat, with a date filter.
- **Routines.** Open a routine to see its details and press Run now. Routine times show the right time zone.
- **Approve in one tap.** Answer a waiting request right inside Needs you, or use Approve all for the stack. A refusal still shows its reason.
- **Errors name the service.** When something holds a reply, the message says which service, such as Flux hold, limit reached or busy.
- **Pictures in chat.** Pictures a bot saves in its workspace show in chat and open full size.

## Bots and rooms in 1.0.18

- **Read your other chats.** A bot can read from your other chats, and you pick the exact excerpts it shares into a room.
- **Post by rule.** A bot can post into a room by a rule you set, and you see each post.
- **Many bots.** The bot rail and lists stay quick with 18 or more bots.
- **Bring what you use.** First run has doors to import a Grok bot or tell a bot about yourself, and saving your name or picking a job answers at once.
- **Clients card.** The Clients card explains how to switch clients.

## Engines in 1.0.18

- **Fuigo 1.0.40.** Fuigo 1.0.40 is the engine on board, and Fuigo asks before it reads the keys and sign-ins you have given Murage.
- **Muse Code.** Muse Code joins the engine list. It runs on a regular model, writes only at Full access, and Stop ends all of its work.
- **Signed-in engines first.** New and paired bots start on an engine you are already signed in to.
- **Flux keys.** When a Flux key does not cover a model, Murage retries on a model the key does cover.
- **Model defaults.** Default models for Claude and Codex are up to date.
- **First Codex turn.** The first Codex turn on a fresh Mac goes straight through.
- **Stop everywhere.** Stop works, and usage is counted, on every engine that is not ACP.

## Murage for Chrome and phone in 1.0.18

- **Guided Chrome setup.** Use my Chrome setup takes you through it in three steps, and the extension picks up again after a restart.
- **Phone tabs.** The phone app has tabs along the bottom and a You sheet with Approve on it.
- **Phone calls.** A Phone calls section in Settings lets a bot place calls for you.

## Settings and money in 1.0.18

- **Dollar meter.** The dollar meter gives you a heads-up at 80 percent of your budget.
- **Plan usage.** The Plan usage card shows what your plan allows.
- **Health.** Health also checks a Flux call in flight, the Fuigo version you have, and screenshots.
- **Voice and calls.** Voice and calls stop when Flux says your budget is spent, and tell you.

## Memory and privacy in 1.0.18

- **Hidden stays hidden.** Memory you have hidden or withheld stays hidden, including when a skill reviews memory.
- **Recall.** Recall says "unknown" when part of a thread's history is too long to walk.
- **Logs.** Logs, and anything sent on from them, leave sensitive text out.
- **Owner-only content.** Content meant for you alone stays out of room transcripts, so a room only shows what you chose to share.
- **Record file.** The file where Murage records which turns touched things outside a bot's folder stays small, and it comes through a crash intact.
- **Engine logs.** Engine logs stay a steady size, and a resumed chat picks up exactly where it left off.

## Updates and backups in 1.0.18

- **Backups.** A backup that needs more disk space waits and tells you, and backup and restore work as a standard Windows user.
- **Mac updates.** Mac updates hand off cleanly, and the splash stays until the window paints.
- **Update notice.** A notice tells you when a new version is ready.

## Better in 1.0.18

- Murage paints its first screen faster.
- Core libraries are brought up to date.
- Plus many smaller improvements throughout.
