## Approve all in 1.0.17

- **Approve all.** Needs you has an Approve all button. It shows what you are about to approve, up to 25 at a time, and each one runs once, as if you tapped Allow once.
- **Big steps wait.** Payments, email, publishing, deleting outside a bot's folder and other big steps stay in your Inbox for you to answer one by one.

## Find things faster in 1.0.17

- **Settings search.** Settings search takes you straight to the setting, opens any folded section around it, and offers to ask your Chief of Staff when nothing matches.
- **Ask about this screen.** In the help panel, Ask about this screen puts your question into your Chief of Staff's chat.
- **Help pages.** Today, Needs you and Health each open their own help page.
- **Tips and hints.** Buttons show a short tip when you point at them or reach them with the keyboard, and a new hint shows once.

## Look and feel in 1.0.17

- **Fonts.** Choose the font Murage uses in Settings.
- **Wide tables.** A reply with a wide table uses the full width of the chat, so you see every column.
- **Bot pictures.** Zoom and move a bot's picture to frame it the way you like.
- **Quicker start.** Group and project rooms open in their own pane while they load, so Murage starts quicker.

## Voice in 1.0.17

- **Voice Library.** With your own ElevenLabs key, search the Voice Library and add a voice from it.
- **Spoken replies.** Spoken replies start cleanly and read straight through.

## Murage for Chrome in 1.0.17

- **Paused browser.** If a bot paused your browser, Murage tells you and shows how to resume.
- **Chrome profiles.** A site you allow is saved to the Chrome profile you picked it from.

## Phone in 1.0.17

- **Clear setup.** The iPhone and Android apps tell you plainly when Share and ask or Phone tools is off on your computer, and where to turn it on.
- **Shared bots.** When a bot is shared, the desktop says that phone previews stay off while it is.

## Better in 1.0.17

- Commands that run PowerShell are checked faster.
- Plus many smaller improvements throughout.
