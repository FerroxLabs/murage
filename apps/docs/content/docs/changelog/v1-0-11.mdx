## Approvals in 1.0.11

- **Your answer is always welcome.** A tool approval card in a chat waits for you on every engine until you answer or the turn stops.
- **Not answered is its own record.** A card that closes without your answer reads Not answered.
- **File patches are read for what they change.** When Fuigo applies a patch, your level applies to what the patch really does. A patch Murage cannot read in full asks first, at every level including No limits.

## Flux in 1.0.11

- **Sign in with Flux is on by default.** It is ready in Models & engines.

## Engines in 1.0.11

- **Grok starts on the model you picked.** Murage sets the model once, waits for Grok's model list when it is still loading, and leaves a session that already runs your pick alone.
- **Local models run your tools** on the OpenAI-compatible engine.
- **Gemini tool calls run whole** through the OpenAI-compatible engine.

## Routines and chats in 1.0.11

- **Results come back to the chat.** A routine's result lands in the chat it was made in. The routine editor can switch this to the Routines list.
- **Bots read their own folders freely.** SOUL.md, skills and memory open without a card at any level.
- **The answering model is visible.** The chat shows a quiet line when the model that answered differs from the one you picked.
- **Obsidian links open Obsidian.**
