Murage 1.0.18 is out for Mac, Windows and Linux, led by No limits: when you start the request yourself, your bot gets on with the job, and Murage keeps a short list of files and places protected.

## No limits, with a protected list

Set a bot to No limits, ask it for something, and it runs the job. Murage protects certain files and places, such as the keys and sign-ins you have given it, and answers a request for one of them with a clear refusal that says why. Every other level and every other kind of turn works as before.

If a bot has taken in something from outside, such as a web page, an email or a file someone sent, only that bot keeps the extra check. Your other bots keep going without it. Don't put a bot on No limits if it can reach customers, money or other people's data.

Commands that run in your Mac temp folders work as they always should at No limits, while the real system folders stay protected. A command pointed at a folder that is not valid gets a clear refusal at every level.

Writing output to /dev/null works, and a trivial inline check, such as a one-number node or python command, runs straight away.

## New

- **Today, two ways.** The Today page has a Calm view for a short summary and a Detailed view for the full list, in every language.
- **Approve in one tap.** Answer a waiting request right inside Needs you, or Approve all for the stack. A refusal still shows its reason.
- **Bots read your other chats.** A bot can read from your other chats, and you pick the exact excerpts it shares into a room. A bot can also post in a room by a rule you set, and you see each post.
- **Muse Code.** Muse Code joins the engine list. It runs on a regular model, writes only at Full access, and Stop ends all of its work.
- **Murage for Chrome, guided.** Use my Chrome setup takes you through it in three steps, and the extension picks up again after a restart.
- **Your phone, closer.** The phone app has tabs along the bottom, and a You sheet with Approve right on it. A Phone calls section in Settings lets a bot place calls for you.

- A Stop you press holds, even when a turn is just starting.
