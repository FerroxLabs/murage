Murage 1.0.11 is out for Mac, Windows and Linux, led by approvals that wait for you and engines that follow your picks.

## Your answer is always welcome
- A tool approval card in a chat waits for you, on every engine, for as long as you need. Fuigo, Grok, Hermes, OpenClaw, Claude, Codex, Pi and the OpenAI-compatible engine all hold the card until you answer or the turn stops.
- A card that closes without your answer reads Not answered, so the record shows what you actually decided.

## New
- **Sign in with Flux is on by default.** It is ready in Models & engines.
- **File patches are read for what they change.** When Fuigo applies a patch, your level applies to what the patch really does. A patch Murage cannot read in full asks first, at every level including No limits.
- **Grok starts on the model you picked.** Murage sets the model once, waits for Grok's own model list when it is still loading, and leaves a session that already runs your pick alone. Stop is honored at every step.

## Better
- A routine's result lands in the chat it was made in, as one card with the reply, the files and Open run. You can switch this to the Routines list in the routine editor.
- A bot reads its own folders, such as its SOUL.md and skills, without a card at any level.
- Most Flux requests carry the bot, the feature and the thread, and the chat shows a quiet line when the model that answered differs from the one you picked.
- Local models on the OpenAI-compatible engine run your tools.
- Settings shows a short reason for any feature that is off, so every card stays in its place.
- Gemini tool calls streamed through the OpenAI-compatible engine run whole.
- Links to Obsidian notes in a chat open Obsidian.
- The bundled tunnel connector is updated to the latest release.

## How to update
- Murage shows an update banner when 1.0.11 is ready. You can also choose Check for updates in Settings.
