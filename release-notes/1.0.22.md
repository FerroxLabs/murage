[[HEADLINE: Your bots remember you, on every chat.]] Murage 1.0.22 is out for Mac, Windows and Linux.

You tell your bot about a client on Monday. On Thursday you open a new chat. [[VERIFY-MEMORY]] Now it already knows.

Don't explain yourself twice.

Update now and try it on your next chat. Ask your bot about something you told it last week, in a different chat, and see what comes back.

### Better

**[[VERIFY-MEMORY]] Memory on every chat.** Each chat brings in what your bots remember about you, from the first message. Your turn starts right away, even while memory is still catching up.

**[[VERIFY-HELDBACK]] Every reply reaches you.** Murage masks only the private text and shows you everything else. So a normal reply lands in full.

**[[VERIFY-CARRYON]] Your turn keeps going.** If memory or the connection takes a moment, your bot carries on with the job.

**[[VERIFY-BACKUP]] Backups run to the end.** Backups that had stalled now run to completion.
[[MAYBE-SEARCH]] **Quick search.** Search stays quick and calm even when your computer is busy.

[[MAYBE-ENGINE]] **Engines ready on Mac.** Every built-in engine now starts reliably on the latest macOS.
