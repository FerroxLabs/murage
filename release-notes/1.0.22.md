# Murage 1.0.22

Step away and your bots catch up on what they remember. Every reply now reaches you in full.

## What's new

- When you step away from Murage, your bots sort through older memories, a few at a time. Nothing is deleted.

## Improvements

- Every reply reaches you: Murage holds back only the private text, so a normal answer comes straight through.
- Your bot's tools keep working while Murage checks its memory, so replies that use them come straight through.
- Ask Murage to forget a memory and it's removed, every time.
- While you're idle, Murage writes very little to your disk, and a memory you asked it to forget is still removed.
- Message search stays quick, even when your computer is busy.
- Backups run through to completion.
- When you remove text, Murage always clears it from its private records.
