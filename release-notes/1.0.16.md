Murage 1.0.16 is out for Mac, Windows and Linux, led by a Health page that tells you what to fix first and a Needs you list you can answer in place.

## Health shows what to fix first
- Health lists what needs your attention in order, Flux and Fuigo first, with one button to fix each and a way to check again.

## Answer right where you are
- Rows in Needs you open in place, so you can answer a bot without leaving the list.
- Every bot replies the same way: the answer first, one question at a time, in plain words and your language.
- A team lead bot can now set up a new section for a client.

## Engines
- Bundled Fuigo moves to 1.0.39.
- Pi tells you when a session is slow to start, and shows what it costs.
- When Codex turns down a call, Murage names the call.
- An engine running under WSL on Windows gets a short note on how it works there.
- Hermes sends each reply once.

## Phone and voice
- Entering the pairing code on your phone is quicker.
- In a call, the transcript matches the words that were spoken.

## Better
- Sign-in headers are masked in diagnostics files, and feedback is cleaned before it is sent.
- If the helper app does not start, Murage tries again on its own.
- Avatar eyes and animation keep moving when Murage is not the window in front.
- A New conversation you queue opens as you asked.
- Plus stability fixes.
