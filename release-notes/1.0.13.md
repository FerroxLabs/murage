Murage 1.0.13 is out for Mac, Windows and Linux, led by a review step before a bot gets more access and a new Health page for your engines.

## Review before more access
- Raising a bot to Full access or No limits shows you the change to review first.

## New
- **Health page.** A new Health page shows each engine's state. Its title and back button stay on screen as you scroll.

## Better
- No limits asks far less often. Your deny rules and wipe list still apply, and Codex edits and read-only PowerShell commands run under it.
- Question cards wait for your answer, and the bot that asked picks the conversation back up when you reply.
- Engines remember your setup answers and keep their settings across restarts.
- Claude chats on Windows pick up where they left off.
- Fuigo checks before it works in a folder you haven't trusted, and Murage answers from the choices you already saved. Murage now includes Fuigo 1.0.37.
- Backups tell you if they stop, and stay inside their space budget.
- A routine's level chip shows the level it actually runs at.
- A conversation you exclude stays excluded.
- Busy rooms keep moving.
- On Mac, the folder access prompt is clearer.
- MCP sign-in failures say what to do next.
- The remote door reconnects when Tailscale starts after Murage.
- Background watchers from older versions retire after an update.
- Plus stability fixes.

## How to update
- Murage shows an update banner when 1.0.13 is ready. You can also choose Check for updates in Settings.
