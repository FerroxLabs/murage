Murage 1.0.8 is out for Mac, Windows and Linux, led by bots that get on with the work you gave them.

## Fewer interruptions at Full access
- At Full access and No limits, your bots ask far less often before everyday commands, most of all on Windows.
- On Windows, everyday commands keep flowing even when Murage's check of your computer's setup is slow or doesn't finish.
- Raise your default and Murage offers to apply it to your open conversations too, with a count of how many it will change.
- No limits in a project room stays on after Murage restarts.

## Stop that lands cleanly
- On Linux, Stop now gives sandboxed engines a moment to finish up and tidy away their work, so the next turn picks up right where you left off.

## Backups you can count on
- If Murage can't save its backup state, it tells you exactly what to check (disk space and permissions), and Return to workspace takes you straight back once it's sorted.

## Your computer stays yours
- On Windows, macOS and Linux x64, engines Murage wraps cannot shut down or restart your computer.

## Your secrets stay hidden
- Keys, tokens and private key blocks are masked in the error messages Murage shows you, from engines, computers, checkpoints and updates.

## On Ubuntu 22.04 and Debian 12
- Deleting a chat now also clears the copy Fuigo keeps on that computer, in under a second.

## Also new
- The built-in browser is on for everyone, and on Mac, Teach a skill is ready to use from the start.
- Approvals a helper bot asks for now reach you and carry its work on once you answer.
- The sidebar now has a single update control: one spinner while a new version downloads, and a tap opens the update details.
- When a reply can't finish, or ends with nothing to show, Murage now tells you why in one plain line instead of leaving the chat quiet.

## How to update
- Murage shows an update banner when 1.0.8 is ready. You can also choose Check for updates in Settings.
