Murage 1.0.17 is out for Mac, Windows and Linux, led by Approve all: answer a stack of waiting requests in one go, while the big steps still wait for you.

## Approve all
- Needs you has an Approve all button. It shows what you are about to approve, up to 25 at a time, and each one runs once, as if you tapped Allow once.
- Payments, email, publishing, deleting outside a bot's folder and other big steps stay in your Inbox for you to answer one by one.

## Find things faster
- Settings search takes you straight to the setting, opens any folded section around it, and offers to ask your Chief of Staff when nothing matches.
- In the help panel, Ask about this screen puts your question into your Chief of Staff's chat.
- Today, Needs you and Health each open their own help page.
- Buttons show a short tip when you point at them or reach them with the keyboard, and a new hint shows once.

## Look and feel
- Choose the font Murage uses in Settings.
- A reply with a wide table uses the full width of the chat, so you see every column.
- Zoom and move a bot's picture to frame it the way you like.
- Group and project rooms open in their own pane while they load, so Murage starts quicker.

## Voice
- With your own ElevenLabs key, search the Voice Library and add a voice from it.
- Spoken replies start cleanly and read straight through.

## Murage for Chrome
- If a bot paused your browser, Murage tells you and shows how to resume.
- A site you allow is saved to the Chrome profile you picked it from.

## Phone
- The iPhone and Android apps tell you plainly when Share and ask or Phone tools is off on your computer, and where to turn it on.
- When a bot is shared, the desktop says that phone previews stay off while it is.

## Better
- Commands that run PowerShell are checked faster.
- Plus many smaller improvements throughout.
