# Murage 1.0.23

Your bots answer the moment you open a chat, and Fuigo starts cleanly on Windows.

## What's new

- Claude, Codex, Fuigo and Grok stay warm for the bots you use, so the first reply of a chat starts at once.
- Add a skill or an MCP connection with a guided flow that checks the connection before it saves.
- Fuigo starts cleanly on Windows: the first turn waits for its memory to finish starting, then answers.
- Send a voice note to a bot.
- Run /compact on a Codex bot to trim a long conversation on demand.
- Your Chief of Staff bot keeps a Today view.
- Models you add to a Flux account show by name in the picker.

## Improvements

- Start-up names the step Murage is on while it opens.
- If macOS refuses one power-protection rule, Murage applies the others and carries on.
- A message you send while a turn is running goes out once, in order, even when the turn ends in a browser check or a memory rebuild.
- A recall view builds without waiting on writes that are not its own.
- Cards stay where you left them: a held row or a recorded arrival keeps its card.
- Answer a card in a room goal routine and the member's turn picks up at once.
- A connected app tells you plainly whether it works before any reply count.
- One browser alert, shown once.
- The + sheet on your phone offers the pickers your phone can use and keeps your pending choice when the window resizes.
- A GitHub star prompt that sits above the sidebar footer and never covers a control.
