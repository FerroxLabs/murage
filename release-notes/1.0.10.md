Murage 1.0.10 is out for Mac, Windows and Linux, led by bots that get on with the work you trust them with.

## Your level means it
- At No limits and Full access, your bots run their routines and your own direct messages at the level you chose, without stopping to ask.

## Better
- Routines keep the level you chose, and so do the bots they hand work to and the follow-up turns they wake.
- Direct messages you send your bot from your own Telegram, Slack or Discord count as you, so they run at the bot's level. You can turn this off in Settings.
- Long file writes run straight through at No limits and Full access.
- Connected apps hold steady on a patchy connection: Murage tries again before it calls them out of reach, so your routines keep their apps.
- No limits still stops for the short never-list, such as erasing a disk, and before a bot reads a key file.

## How to update
- Murage shows an update banner when 1.0.10 is ready. You can also choose Check for updates in Settings.
