### Better

**Your bots catch up on memory.** When you step away from Murage, your bots sort through older memories, a few at a time. Nothing is deleted.

**Every reply reaches you.** Murage holds back only the private text and shows you everything else. So a normal answer comes straight through.

**Tools keep working.** Your bot's tools keep working while Murage checks its memory, so replies that use them come straight through.

**Forgetting runs smoothly.** Ask Murage to forget a memory and it's removed, every time.

**Light on your disk while you're away.** While you're idle, Murage writes very little to your disk. A memory you asked it to forget is still removed.

**Search stays quick.** Message search keeps its pace, even when your computer is busy.

**Backups run to the end.** Your backups run through to completion.

**Removed text is always cleared.** When you remove text, Murage always clears it from its private records.
