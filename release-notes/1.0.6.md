# Murage 1.0.6

This update brings All chats, bots that know where they belong, steadier Fuigo starts on Windows and quicker memory upkeep on long histories.

## What's new

- All chats: every conversation in one searchable list, opened from a wider chat pop-up.
- Your bots know which projects and groups they belong to.
- Faster, steadier starts for Fuigo on Windows, from every install shape.
- A smoother first run: an app connects under the label you confirm.
- Clearer model chips, and memories your bots learn from you are anchored to your own words.

## Improvements

- At Full access, your own scripts on disk, like `bash build.sh`, run straight through. Ask and Auto still check with you first.
- Forget a memory and its words are gone the moment you click.
- A reply a teammate hands back waits for its check, then arrives in the right conversation.
- A Codex that is slow to start says so, and is never called out of date.
- On a desktop with no internet, chats keep their live updates from your own computer.
- Bots look through your past chats without stopping to ask, and a line you forgot never turns up.
- Deleting conversations on long histories finishes faster, and Stop on a routine ends it cleanly.
- Account and card numbers you paste are masked in more formats, IBANs included.
- Downloads you keep from the browser are reported as kept, in plain words.
- Backups know every folder Murage keeps, so they run straight through.
- Library cards read as short, plain descriptions of what each template does for you.
