Murage 1.0.14 is out for Mac, Windows and Linux, led by one-button engine sign-in and Fuigo 1.0.38.

## Sign in with one button
- Signing in to an engine is one button in Settings. No terminal and no commands to copy.
- Ask Ember in chat on your computer to sign you in and it gives you that same button.
- While Murage is still checking a sign-in, Health and Today say "Checking sign-in" until it knows.
- Needs you only asks you to sign in to engines your bots or routines use, and first run sets you up on Fuigo and Flux.

## Voice notes
- A bot's voice note now plays on your phone and in the browser, as well as on your computer.
- A voice note or file a bot sends partway through a turn now sits in its reply, not inside Worked for.

## Fuigo 1.0.38
- Trust a folder during a conversation and Fuigo brings in its project instructions at the next turn. Conversations from before the update stay as they were, so start a new one. Nested AGENTS.md files and project skills arrive with your next new conversation. Murage now includes Fuigo 1.0.38.
- On Flux Router, Fuigo trusts the fresh work folder Murage makes for a new chat, so a new chat starts straight away. A folder you pick yourself still asks.
- A folder's .envrc file never runs inside Fuigo on a bot turn.

## Better
- When Murage can't read a command, its approval card now says exactly that, and asks you before it runs.
- On Windows, Murage reads a PowerShell command split across lines as one command, so it gets the same approval checks as the same command on one line.
- A message queued for the next turn shows its images and files as chips, not raw tags.
- If Murage quits or restarts during a Codex, Pi, Antigravity or OpenAI tools turn, that turn now shows it didn't finish, and the bot waiting on it hears back when Murage starts again.
- Stop All on question cards past the follow-up limit ends them as stopped.
- On your phone, Murage keeps its screen after a reconnect.
- Health shows a working Fuigo as ready while the page loads, and its dot matches Needs you during first run.
- Settings > Storage lists only files you still have.
- Saving an MCP command picks up the latest saved copy.
- Linux without a keyring: Settings now tells you up front why a Flux Router key can't be saved yet, and the two ways forward.
- Plus stability fixes.

## How to update
- Murage shows an update banner when 1.0.14 is ready. You can also choose Check for updates in Settings.
