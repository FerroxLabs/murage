Murage 1.0.20 is out for Mac, Windows and Linux, with one calm place to reconnect your apps and conversations that carry on smoothly from first message to last.

Reconnect your apps from a single line, pick up right where you left off after a restart, and let updates install on their own schedule.

## Better

- **Reconnect connected apps in one calm place.** A single line lists the apps that need you to sign in again, with a row for each app. Put it away with X or Later, and it comes back once a day, or after a restart, until you reconnect. The way back to reconnect is always there.
- Notices in the corner of the window arrive one at a time, in order, so Undo, team, update and install messages never cover your composer.
- Murage opens again after your Mac restarts or joins a different network. It recognizes its own data folder by your Mac's identity, so you go straight back to work.
- Replies mention left-out memory only when something was actually left out. A bot that recalls by words alone searched everything it could read, so its replies stay clean.
- Your conversation stays readable while an engine starts and when you switch engines. A brand new chat shows your first reply in full, approvals keep their full history, and each owner transcript stays separate.
- Setting up your team keeps your other conversations in place: giving a new bot a section lead role leaves every other chat's memory as it was.
- When a turn has two texts, Murage names the step between them, so you can follow what the bot did.
- When an engine stops without giving a reason, the error card says what happened in plain words, and a second Murage profile open at the same time tells you when you quit.
- Updates hand over cleanly: Murage pauses its timed launch jobs and takes them down before it restarts to install, so the new version starts fresh.
- Murage starts quickly after a restart. The one-time index of your conversations builds once and carries on after launch instead of starting over each time.
- Murage picks up cleanly after an unexpected quit, releasing what the earlier session held and retrying the start step once.
- Deleting a bot's data waits only for other programs. Murage's own engine processes no longer hold it up, and the "still open" line appears once for each program that has the folder open.
- Stop ends a turn quietly: a confirmed Stop leaves a clean chat with no extra message after it.
- Low-disk messages read naturally in all 8 languages.

## How to update

Murage shows an update banner when 1.0.20 is ready, or choose Check for updates in Settings.
