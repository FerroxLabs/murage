# Automatic mode

Murage starts in Automatic mode. Your bots read, write and run things in their own folders and on your computer without stopping
to ask you first.

## What always asks

A few things always ask, at every level, because they are hard to undo or reach other people:

- Deleting a lot at once, like a whole folder or drive
- Spending money because a web page, email or file told it to
- Messaging someone new, or someone other than you
- Posting where other people will see it

Reading never asks, at any level.

## Ask first

If you would rather approve each action that changes something, click **Automatic** under the message box to switch that
conversation to **Ask first**, or open Settings, pick the bot and choose **Ask first** for its new conversations. One click switches
back to **Automatic** at any time (it asks you to confirm first).

The arrow next to it, and Settings > the bot > **Advanced**, also have Auto (asks about more than Automatic) and No limits (asks about less).

## Upgrading

If you used Murage before this version, nothing changed: every bot keeps the level it had, and you will not see the first-run notice above the message box.
